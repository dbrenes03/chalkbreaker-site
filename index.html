Chalkbreaker

Fantasy Football Decision Intelligence

Chalkbreaker is a private fantasy football decision-intelligence application currently in MVP development for personal use.

The project is designed to help analyze league context, rosters, weekly matchups, player availability, waiver opportunities, and trade scenarios using read-only fantasy league data together with independently sourced football statistics and analytics.

Chalkbreaker is currently being developed as a web/PWA application and is not publicly available for use.

Read-only fantasy data integration
Chalkbreaker is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yahoo.
